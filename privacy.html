Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026
BassWave processes tab audio locally in your browser. It does not upload, record, sell, or share your audio. No account, analytics, advertising identifiers, or third-party tracking services are used.

What BassWave accesses

When you explicitly enable BassWave for an eligible Chrome tab, the extension accesses that tab’s audio stream to apply bass gain, presets, compression, and soft clipping. It also reads the active tab’s title, favicon URL, and URL to show which tab you are controlling, block restricted pages, and stop processing after cross-origin navigation.

BassWave cannot capture Chrome Web Store pages, chrome:// pages, file:// pages, other restricted browser pages, or a tab whose audio is already being captured by another extension.

How information is used

  • Tab audio is used only to provide the bass-processing effect you request and play the processed audio back to you.
  • The tab title and site favicon are displayed in the popup so you can confirm which tab you are controlling.
  • The tab URL is checked for restricted-page handling and navigation cleanup. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or analytics.

What is stored

BassWave stores the following information locally in your Chrome profile:

  • Your consent to local audio processing.
  • Your last-used preset, bass gain, Smooth Peaks preference, and sound-engine choice in chrome.storage.local.
  • Per-tab processing status, URL, generation token, and last error in chrome.storage.session. Session data is cleared when the browser session ends.

Audio is not written to disk, stored in extension storage, or retained after processing stops.

Data sharing and third parties

BassWave does not transmit tab audio, URLs, titles, favicons, settings, or usage data off your device. It does not use cookies, analytics, telemetry, remote code, advertising services, or third-party APIs. The extension does not sell data or use it for creditworthiness, lending, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

Your choices and controls

You choose when to enable processing for a tab. Turn BassWave off, close the tab, or navigate to another origin to stop processing. Removing the extension removes its locally stored settings and consent. You can also clear site data for the extension through Chrome’s extension settings.

Data retention and deletion

Preset, gain, Smooth Peaks, sound-engine, and consent preferences remain in local extension storage until you remove the extension or clear its data. Active-tab session state lasts only for the current browser session. Audio exists only in the live browser audio pipeline while processing is active.

Changes to this policy

If BassWave’s data practices change, this policy will be updated and the revision date will change. Material changes will be communicated through the product website or Chrome Web Store listing before they take effect.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, open an issue at github.com/ggrp-byte/BassWave/issues.
