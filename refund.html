Refund Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026
BassWave is free. There is no paid version, subscription, license key, or in-app purchase, so no payment can be charged through the extension.

1. No purchases

All BassWave features are available at no cost after installation from the Chrome Web Store. The extension does not include a checkout flow, paid tiers, or trial upgrades.

2. Refunds

Because BassWave does not sell anything, there is no purchase to refund. If you believe a third party charged you for BassWave, contact us with the details so we can help you identify the source.

3. Support

If you experience a technical issue, open an issue at github.com/ggrp-byte/BassWave/issues. We will do our best to help, and we may fix the problem in a future update.

4. Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue at github.com/ggrp-byte/BassWave/issues.
