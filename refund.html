Refund Policy

Last updated: July 2025

BassWave is a free Chrome extension. All core features are available at no cost. If you choose to purchase a Pro license, the following refund policy applies:

1. Free version

The free version of BassWave does not require payment and therefore is not eligible for refunds. You may stop using the extension at any time.

2. Pro license

Pro licenses are sold as a one-time, lifetime purchase. Due to the digital nature of the product and immediate access upon purchase, all sales are final. No refunds are offered after a Pro license purchase.

3. Before purchasing

We encourage you to test all free features thoroughly before purchasing a Pro license. If you experience technical issues, please contact us via GitHub Issues and we will do our best to help.
