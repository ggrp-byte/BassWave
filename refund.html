Refund Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Short version: if BassWave Pro doesn't work for you, contact us within 14 days of purchase and we'll refund you — no questions asked.

14-day money-back guarantee

We offer a full refund within 14 days of your purchase date, no questions asked. If you're not satisfied with BassWave Pro for any reason, just contact us and we'll process your refund promptly.

How to request a refund

Send an email to contact@basswave.app with:

  • The email address you used to purchase
  • Your order number (found in your purchase confirmation email)

We'll process your refund within 3–5 business days. The money will be returned to your original payment method.

After 14 days

Refunds requested after the 14-day window are evaluated on a case-by-case basis. If BassWave Pro has a technical issue that we cannot fix, we will issue a refund regardless of when the purchase was made.

What is not refundable

  • Purchases made more than 14 days ago where the product worked as described
  • Partial refunds for partial use of the Pro tier

Contact

Questions? Email us at contact@basswave.app — we typically respond within 24 hours on business days.
