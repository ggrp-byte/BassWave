1. The extension

BassWave shapes the bass and clarity of eligible Chrome tabs using locally processed Web Audio. It includes five presets, a manual bass-gain slider, Smooth Peaks, independent tab controls, and a choice between the 2.2 and 2.1 Classic sound engine. All features are available at no cost.

2. Eligibility and compatibility

BassWave works only with tabs that are playing audio and that Chrome allows the extension to capture. It cannot process chrome:// pages, file:// pages, the Chrome Web Store, other restricted browser pages, or a tab whose audio is already captured by another extension.
